SafeHer
A Smart Women's Safety Platform

SafeHer is a women's safety platform developed as part of a GTU Societal Internship project. It helps women stay safe by providing emergency SOS support, real-time location sharing, secure incident reporting, nearby emergency services, and cybersecurity-based protection for user data.

Empowering Women Through Safety Tech

SafeHer was proposed after conducting a field survey at different public places in Mehsana, including GSRTC Bus Stand, Raj Rajeshwari Garden, Sarvajanik Panchlimdi School, Aalay Paper Pack, and Mehsana Railway Junction.

The survey revealed that many women experience problems such as unwanted staring, harassment, poor lighting, lack of security, and hesitation in reporting incidents.

Based on these observations, SafeHer was proposed as a secure platform that combines emergency assistance, location-based services, secure reporting, and cybersecurity techniques to improve women's safety while protecting users' personal information.

Dignity & Freedom

Safety is not just about protection; it is about the freedom to study, work, and travel at any hour without fear.

Survey Conducted

The proposed SafeHer platform is based on a field survey conducted at five different public locations in Mehsana.

GSRTC Bus Stand

High footfall area with common safety and observation gaps noted in evening hours.

Raj Rajeshwari Garden

Public park zone where visitors highlighted sparse lighting on pathways.

Sarvajanik School

Educational vicinity surveyed to gather feedback from students and parents.

Aalay Paper Pack

Industrial area assessed to capture feedback from working women during shifts.

Railway Junction

Transit hub surveyed to assess passenger safety, transit support, and help desks.

More than 30 women from different age groups participated in the survey. Their valuable feedback helped identify common safety concerns and inspired the development of SafeHer.

Core Platform Features

Emergency SOS Alert

One-click trigger to immediately share live location coordinates with pre-configured guardians.

Real-Time Location Sharing

Provide continuous location tracing path to your guardians with end-to-end encryption support.

Secure Incident Reporting

Report harassment, staring, or unsafe sites anonymously. Upload photo evidence securely.

Safety Risk Map

Visual overlay of unsafe hotspots color-coded (Red/Yellow/Green) based on verified public incidents.

Nearby Police Stations

Instant map search and contact directories of police stations nearest to your current location.

Emergency Helpline

One-tap dialing access to 112, 181, and 1091 emergency women care organizations.

Trusted Contacts

Configure up to 5 trusted guardians to receive automatic security pings during travel.

Secure Login & Verification

Access dashboard protected with 2FA/OTP login validation and password hashing layers.

Data Encryption

User records and reports encrypted in transport and storage using AES and bcrypt standards.
