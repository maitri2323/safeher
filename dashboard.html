Daily Safety Tips & Awareness
1. Know Your Surroundings

Map out routes before travel. Use the SafeHer Area Map to identify safe public paths with high surveillance and police presence.

2. Keep SOS Active

Ensure that your mobile phone's GPS is enabled. Keep the SOS shortcut active and verify that your Trusted Guardian contacts are up to date.

3. Trust Your Instincts

If a location or path feels uncomfortable, do not hesitate to change direction or activate the live location sharing feature with your friend/family.

4. Stay in Well-lit Zones

Avoid shortcuts through dark alleys or alleys known to be empty. Use main public avenues near commercial zones whenever walking alone.
